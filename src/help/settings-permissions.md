Permissions shows whether Brainstead has Full Disk Access. Notifications and automation are asked for when a feature first needs them, not at startup.

## Give Brainstead Full Disk Access
If your vault lives in a cloud-synced folder, macOS without Full Disk Access asks separately for each protected place Brainstead reads, or blocks it. To grant it:

1. Click **Open System Settings**.
2. Under Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access, turn Brainstead on.
3. If macOS offers to quit and reopen Brainstead, say yes.

The card checks again when Brainstead's window comes to the front, or at once with **Check again**. Once granted, the card says so and the buttons go. An assistant can tell whether Brainstead has it.
