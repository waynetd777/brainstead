Five ways to find what you need in the vault.

## Jump anywhere with ⌘K
Press `⌘K` and type. Before you type it lists the screens, two to a row with each one's key; the arrow keys move between them. The best matching files come first, then open tasks, screens and commands. `Tab` limits the list to notes, wiki or sources files. `↩` opens the selected item; `⌘↩` takes what you typed to the [Search](app:search) page.

## Search the whole vault
On [Search](app:search) (`⌘⇧F`), words match in any form. Use `+word` to require a word, `-word` to leave it out, `"two words"` for a phrase and `tag:hiring` for a tag. `since:2026-09` limits by date. The chips and menus narrow by layer and date. **Save search** keeps a search you run often.

## Filter a list as you type
The box at the top of [Notes](app:notes), Wiki, Sources and Templates filters that list, and the one above [Tasks](app:tasks) narrows its lists. Each list's box searches the text as well as the names; `↑` and `↓` step through the matches and `↩` opens one. A note opened from a search has every match marked; `⌘F` finds within it.

## Follow the links
An open note lists what links to it under **Linked from**, beside the note. Its **Graph** button opens the [Graph](app:graph) centred on it: click a bubble to centre on that page, double-click to open it, and raise the depth to see links of links.

## See what changed
[Activity](app:activity) shows a heatmap of files changed each day and the log of Brainstead's own changes. Click a day to see the files that changed on it. Search the log, or filter it by kind of change.
