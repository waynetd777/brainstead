Notes sets how you write and how notes and wiki pages look.

## Check spelling and grammar
**Check spelling** puts a red wavy line under words macOS doesn't know as you write; click one for guesses, Learn or Ignore. **Check grammar** puts a blue wavy line where macOS sees a grammar problem; click it for the explanation and fixes. Both are on unless you switch them off.

**Spelling language** chooses which English words are checked against, such as colour or color. It lists the English dictionaries macOS has, and starts as macOS is set. An assistant names them as this menu does, such as British English.

## Read notes aloud
**Read aloud** chooses the **Voice** notes are read in (English voices, best first), the **Speed** (a slider from 0.5× to 2×), and whether to **Highlight each word** (off tints the whole paragraph instead). **Test** says a sentence in that voice and speed.

## Choose how documents look
**Document look** sets the theme and colour for notes, wiki pages and their PDFs. The themes are Brainstead, Business, Editorial, Modern, Minimal, Report and Technical; hover over one to see what it's like. The swatches below set the accent colour. The sample under them shows the result.

To change one note's look, do it from that note's screen. If any notes have their own look, **Notes with their own look** says how many, and **Use the defaults for all** makes them all follow these settings again. An assistant can list those notes and use the defaults for all too, when you ask.

## Make documents light or dark
**Light or dark** sets documents to **As the app**, **Light** or **Dark**, so you can read light notes in a dark app or the other way round. PDFs always print light. Changing **Appearance** in [Settings › General](app:settings/general) sets this back to **As the app**.

## Point templates at their scripts
New note runs the templates in the vault's `Templates` folder, written in Templater syntax. Their user scripts (`tp.user`) are the `.js` files in the folder named here, `Templates/scripts` unless you change it. Type a folder inside the vault; it's saved when you leave the box. **Templater docs ↗** opens Templater's documentation in your browser.
