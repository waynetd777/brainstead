Draft reply reads a thread, checks whether it answered what was asked, and drafts a reply grounded in your vault. Brainstead never sends anything: you copy the reply into Outlook or Teams. Open it from **Draft a reply** in a captured thread's top bar, `D` on an email or chat capture in the [Inbox](app:inbox), ⌘K, or Ask's `/` picker when your vault has a draft-reply skill.

## Choose the thread
Pick a captured Outlook or Teams thread from the list beside **Thread**; the newest are first, each named by its subject and day ("Re Orbit App launch date · Fri 2 Oct"; Teams chats start "Teams:"). The thread shows formatted, as in a note. Threads get there through the capture extensions (see [Sources](app:sources)). For a thread you haven't captured, choose **Paste a thread…** and paste the messages, newest last, with who said what. **Open the capture** opens the captured file.

## Draft the reply
Choose a tone, **Brief**, **Warm** or **Formal**, and click **Draft**. The AI reads the thread, looks up the people and topics in your vault, and writes a plain-text reply. Changing the tone afterwards drafts it again; **Redraft** does the same in the same tone.

Under the thread, a verdict says whether the thread answered the question, partly answered it or didn't, with each point asked and whether it was answered.

## Finish and copy
Edit the draft in place. **To fill in** lists the gaps the reply leaves for you, and a line names the vault pages it drew on.

**Copy reply** copies plain text; **Copy as rich text** keeps the paragraphs for an email. When the reply commits you to something, **Add task** captures that follow-up as a task, linked to the captured thread.
