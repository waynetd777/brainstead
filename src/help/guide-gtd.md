This guide takes you once round the GTD loop, from catching a task to the weekly review.

## Capture a task from anywhere
Press `⌃⌥Space` in any app, type the task and press `↩`; it waits in the [Inbox](app:inbox). [Capture and write](help:guide-capture) has the details.

## Clarify the Inbox
Open the [Inbox](app:inbox). Everything you've captured waits there until you decide what it is. For each item press `N` for a next action, `P` for a new project, `W` for waiting for, `2` if you can do it in two minutes, `S` for someday, `R` to file it, or `⌫` to delete. `J` and `K` move between items, and ⌘Z undoes a choice.

## Give every project a next action
Open [Projects](app:projects). A project is a `Project. <name>.md` note; **New project** makes one. Each active project shows its next actions and waiting-fors. A red flag means it has no next action: open it and type one into **Add a next action**. An amber flag means nothing has happened for two weeks.

## Work from Today and Tasks
Start the day on [Today](app:today): overdue tasks, tasks due today, deferred tasks that are back, and what you're waiting for. Use [Tasks](app:tasks) for everything else: pick a context such as `@calls` or an effort of 15 minutes or less to find what fits the moment. Tick tasks as you finish them; ⌘Z undoes a tick.

## Review every week
On the review's day, **Start** appears on Today's To process card; or click **Weekly review** in the sidebar, then **Start the review**. The [Weekly review](app:weekly) walks through Get clear, Get current and Get creative over your real lists. **Pause** keeps your place, and **Finish and save** writes the week's review note.
