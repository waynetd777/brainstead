## Read the day at a glance
Under the greeting, four counts show **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Deferred** and **Waiting**. Overdue turns red when there are any; a count of zero is muted. Click a count to jump to its band in the table below.

The table has a band per section: Overdue (a due date before today), Due today, Deferred until now (a defer date of today or earlier) and Waiting for (tasks tagged `#waiting-for`). Empty bands are hidden, and "Nothing due today" shows when there's nothing due or deferred. While Brainstead is still reading the vault (the first time can take a minute), a banner at the top says so and the cards show "Loading …" until it's done. If the tasks or a side card can't be read after that, it says "Couldn't load …" with **Retry** instead. **All tasks** at the foot opens [Tasks](app:tasks).

## Work through today's tasks
Each row works as it does on [Tasks](help:tasks): tick it, right-click it or use ⋯ for its menu, and click the note name to open its note. The task-list keys work too; `↩` opens the task's note.

When today's tasks carry contexts, a row of chips (**All**, `@calls`, `@office`…) sits at the top of the table. Pick one to show only the tasks in that context, Waiting for included; the counts follow. Click it again, or **All**, to see everything.

## Chase what you're waiting for
Waiting for rows show how long each has waited, such as "4 d waiting" ("since today" on the first day), counted from the task's created date (`➕ 2026-10-01`), and turn amber after a week. A task without a created date shows no count.

Hover over a row, or tab to it, to show the **Draft nudge** button. It opens [Ask](app:ask) with a request for a short, friendly follow-up to copy into Outlook or Teams. Nothing is sent: you copy the draft yourself.

## Capture without leaving the screen
The box under the counts is the same as Quick capture. Type a task and press `↩` to put it on the To Do list, or press Tab to switch to Thought and save it to the Scratchpad. `due: fri` and `defer: +3d` become dates as you type.

**Capture** in the top bar opens the floating Quick capture window, the same one the global shortcut opens. **Ask**, beside `?`, starts a chat in [Ask](app:ask) about your day.

## Use the To process card
The To process card on the right shows what your routine needs:

- **Inbox to clarify** with its count and **Clarify**, which opens the [Inbox](app:inbox). It says the Inbox is clear when it is.
- **Bookmarks to triage** when bookmarks are stale or missing, and **Wiki issues to decide** when Knowledge health has decisions for you.
- **Weekly review day** with **Start** from the weekly review's time on its day until the end of the next day (Friday 16:00 unless you change it in [Settings › Jobs & schedule](app:settings/jobs)), or **Weekly review, paused** with **Carry on** while a review is paused. Either goes straight into the [Weekly review](app:weekly), past its start page.

## Read the daily and weekly summaries
The **Daily summary** card shows the latest daily summary, today's or the most recent one. It's folded to one line: the summary's date and whether a run is going or when the next is due. Click the line to open it and read the summary; click again to fold it. Brainstead remembers which you chose. **Open note** opens the summary note at that day's heading.

Above it, the **Weekly summary** card works the same way for the most recent weekly summary. Both cards show when **Brainstead runs the daily and weekly summaries** is on in [Settings › Jobs & schedule](app:settings/jobs), or when there's a summary to show.

## Pick up where you left off
**Changes held for you** counts every change held in [Changes](app:review) and lists the first four; click one, or **Review**, to accept or reject them. The card is hidden when nothing is held.

**Continue where you left off** shows up to six notes: any with unsaved changes first (marked Unsaved, with **Discard** to throw the changes away), then notes you opened lately, then notes changed lately here or in another editor. **More in Activity** opens [Activity](app:activity).
