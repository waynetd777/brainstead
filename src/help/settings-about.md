About shows which Brainstead you're running and where it keeps its own files.

## Find the version and app data folder
**Version** gives the version number and build. **App data** is the folder where Brainstead keeps its own files, outside the vault: the index, the logs, the daily and weekly summaries' runs, Ask's chats until you save them (and a saved chat's new turns while the vault is read-only), and `summaries/automated.json` if you have one.

## Find the logs
**The app's logs** are what Brainstead noted while running, one file a day in the app data folder's logs. Choose how long to **Keep** them: 7, 14, 30 or 90 days, 14 unless you change it. Older files are deleted. **Show in Finder** opens the folder, which is useful when reporting a problem.
