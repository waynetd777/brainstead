Activity shows the vault's `log.md` newest first, under a heatmap of how many files changed each day, with charts of your notes beside them. Open it from the foot of the sidebar.

## Read the heatmap
Each square is a day, in a column per week starting on Monday, with the months labelled. The darker the square, the more notes, wiki pages and sources last changed that day; hover one for its count. Templates aren't counted, so the total can be lower than the sidebar's files. The chevrons at either end page to earlier and later weeks.

Click a day to filter the log to it. A card above the log then lists the files that last changed that day, with the time; click one to open it. **Clear the day** goes back to the whole log.

## The notes at a glance
On the right, **Notes by type** and **Notes by tag** are pie charts of your notes: the five most common types or tags, then the rest as one slice. The legend beside each names every slice with its count and share. Click a slice or its row (other than the "other tags" one) to see those notes: a type opens [Notes](app:notes) filtered to it, a tag searches for it. **Most linked** lists the notes and wiki pages the most other files link to, with how many; click one to open it.

## Read the log
The log lists every change Brainstead makes to the vault: ingests, assistants' changes, Knowledge health fixes, the daily and weekly summaries, name fixes and the daily check's catalogue update. Your own edits, captures and dropped files aren't logged.

Entries are banded by day, each band with its count; click a band to show that day. An entry's title opens its page when it names one, and hovering the entry shows its description.

## Filter and search the log
The chips filter by kind of change, such as **Ingested**, or **Summary** for a daily or weekly summary; **All** shows every kind. Type in **Search activity** to search titles, descriptions and kinds (an assistant can filter the log the same two ways); while you type, **Best match** or **Latest** orders the matches. While searching, `↑` and `↓` move through the matches, `↩` opens the entry's page (or shows its day) and `Esc` clears the box.
