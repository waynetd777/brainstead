## Capture a task from anywhere
Press `⌃⌥Space` in any app to open Quick capture. Type the task and press `↩`; it lands on the To Do list under Other and the window closes. `due: fri`, `defer: +3d` and `@calls` become a due date, a defer date and a context. Read-only must be off in [Settings › Vault](app:settings/vault) for captures to save.

## Catch a thought in the Scratchpad
In the same box, press Tab to switch to **Thought**. A thought can run to several lines (`⇧↩` for a new one) and goes to the Scratchpad under the date and time. Tasks and thoughts both wait in the [Inbox](app:inbox) until you decide what they are. The same box sits on [Today](app:today).

## Write a new note
Press `⌘N` for New note. Pick a template from the list, or start from an empty note with its type, title and date, and Brainstead names the file for you. The note opens in [Notes](app:notes). `⌘1`, `⌘2` and `⌘3` switch between View, Edit and Source.

## Link and tag as you write
While editing, type `[[` to link another note and `#` to add a tag; suggestions come from the vault. A task line (`- [ ] Send Lena the brief`) takes the same shorthand as capture, such as `due: tomorrow`, and shows up on [Tasks](app:tasks) straight away.

## Save, or keep a draft
Press `⌘S` to save. If you leave a note with unsaved changes, **Keep as draft** keeps them. Drafts show first under Continue where you left off on [Today](app:today), marked Unsaved, with **Discard**.
