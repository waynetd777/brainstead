Graph draws pages as bubbles and links as lines. It updates as the vault changes.

## Centre the graph on a page
Click **Graph** in an open note's top bar to see the graph around it. Or type in **Find a page to centre on** and pick a page. Opened from the sidebar for the first time, Graph shows the whole wiki.

**Neighbourhood** shows the centre page with what it links to and what links to it. The **Depth** slider (1 to 3, 2 to start) takes in links of links; an assistant's `graph` reads the same neighbourhood, of a page named as you'd link it, and an assistant can open Graph around a page. **Whole wiki** shows every wiki page. Back and forward (`⌘[`, `⌘]`) step through the pages you centred on.

## Move around
- Hover a bubble to name it and dim everything but its neighbours. Only the centre and the biggest bubbles are named otherwise.
- Click a bubble to centre on it (a page not written yet shows in the side pane instead); double-click to open the page.
- Drag the background, or scroll with two fingers, to pan; drag a bubble to move it.
- The graph fits the window until you pan, zoom or drag.
- Pinch, or scroll with `⌘` held, to zoom. **Fit** fits the graph to the window again.

A large neighbourhood is cut to the nearest 400 pages; the line under the graph says so, and so does an assistant reading the links around a page.

## Read the colours
Concepts, entities and other wiki pages, sources and summaries, and notes each have their own colour, the same as on the Wiki pages by type pie on Knowledge health: entities blue, sources and summaries orange, concepts green, other wiki pages amber, and notes purple; the chips at the top left name them. A grey ring is a page that's linked to but not written yet; it's labelled too. A bubble's size is how many links it has across the vault.

Click a chip to hide or show that kind; the centre page always stays.

## Use the side pane
With a centre page, the pane names it, says how many links it has and lists the pages linked to or from it; click one to centre on it, or **Open** to open the centre page. On the whole wiki, the pane shows the number of pages and links, and the most linked pages.
