# Start here

> [!example] Example note
> This vault was made with a few example notes so you can see how Brainstead works. Read them, try things on them, then delete them when you're done.

Brainstead is for Getting Things Done, writing notes and keeping a wiki, all in this folder of plain markdown files. Every note here is a `.md` file you can open in any markdown editor.

## The examples

- [[Me. To Do List]]: the master task list. New tasks land under **Other** and wait in the Inbox until you decide what they are.
- [[Me. Scratchpad]]: quick thoughts, each under a date and time. They wait in the Inbox too.
- [[Project. Orbit App launch]]: a project, with its outcome, next actions and what it's waiting for. It shows on the Projects screen.
- [[Meeting. Orbit App kick-off - {{date-1}}]]: a meeting note with actions, a link to a wiki page and a Mermaid diagram.
- [[Guide. What a note can do]]: callouts, maths, tables, tags and a live task query.
- [[Orbit App]] and [[Soft launch]]: wiki pages, kept in `wiki/`, with every claim quoted from a source.
- [[Orbit App brief]] and [[Launch notes from Maya]]: sources, kept in `sources/`. The first has been ingested into the wiki; try **Ingest into the wiki** on the second.
- `Templates/`: example templates. Press `⌘N` to make a note from one, or open Templates to read them:
  - **Meeting**: asks what the meeting is about and names the note.
  - **Daily note**: today's note, linked to yesterday's and tomorrow's, with the **End of day** snippet (`Templates/Snippets/`) pulled in.
  - **1-1**: pick the person from a list; the note gets their name and the date, and a `#followup` task for them.
  - **Project**: asks for the name, outcome and first next action, and makes a note that shows on Projects.
  - **Weekly plan**: a heading per working day, written by a loop, and a line from a script in `Templates/scripts/`.
  - **Reading note**: a book or an article, with its properties, filed in a `Reading` folder.
- `index.md` and `log.md`: the wiki's catalogue and its record of changes, which Brainstead keeps up to date.

## Try these

- [ ] Tick this task, then press `⌘Z` to untick it
- [ ] Press `⌃⌥Space` in any app to capture a task, then clarify it in the Inbox
- [ ] Open Tasks and drag a task into a new order
- [ ] Press `⌘K` and type "orbit" to find everything about the Orbit App
- [ ] Open the `?` at the top right of any screen for its help

## When you're done

Delete the example notes: this one, the project, the meeting, the guide, the two wiki pages, the two sources and the templates (with `Templates/Snippets/` and `Templates/scripts/`). Keep `Me. To Do List`, `Me. Scratchpad`, `Me. Bookmarks`, `index.md` and `log.md`, which Brainstead uses, and delete the example tasks and thoughts in them. You can add the examples back from Settings › Vault.
